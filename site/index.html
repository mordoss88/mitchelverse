    About this map

    A free fan project by a reader of David Mitchell's books. It is not connected to the author or his publishers. All text is in my own words, and all drawings and icons are original. The map can contain mistakes, so please tell me when you find one.

    Turn on AI portraits?

    The portraits of the people are made with an AI image tool. They are not official and they are not from the books.

    They show one fan's idea of each person. They are probably not how you imagined them when you read the books.

    The books give few details about how people look, so hair, faces and clothes are often guesses. If a portrait looks wrong to you, tell us with the form at the bottom of the page.
